* TorrServer
	- Now requires a newer GLIBC than offered by QTS. :(
	- i64 (siena) not starting TorrServer due to GLIBC version error:
		--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
		/share/CACHEDEV1_DATA/.qpkg/TorrServer/repo-cache/torrserver.package: /lib/libc.so.6: version `GLIBC_2.34' not found (required by /share/CACHEDEV1_DATA/.qpkg/TorrServer/repo-cache/torrserver.package)
		--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
		- Not good. libc 2.34 isn't part of any QTS version, and isn't installable through Entware (only has 2.27 available). Looks like the end-of-the-road for TorrServer. :(
	- Still launches fine on a64 (QTS 5.2.6.3195 with libc 2.23). Maybe devs are only compiling i64 version for a later libc? Wait and see if the other arches change this too.
